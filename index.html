    Before you pay: changing your return date

    The live fares above are the cheapest fare type each airline sells, and that type is usually the least flexible. If you may need to move your return, pick one of the recommended fare types on the airline's own site. Changeable fares cost more than the prices shown.

    Singapore Airlines

    Economy Lite
    No voluntary date changes.
    Economy Value
    Date changes for a fee plus any fare difference.
    Economy Standard
    Lower change fee.
    Economy Flexi
    No change fee; fare difference only.

    Qantas
