    Before you pay: changing your return date

    The live fares above are the cheapest fare type each airline sells, and that type is usually the least flexible. If you may need to move your return, pick one of the recommended fare types on the airline's own site. Changeable fares cost more than the prices shown.

    Singapore Airlines

    Economy Lite
    No voluntary date changes.
    Economy Value
    Date changes for a fee plus any fare difference.
    Economy Standard
    Lower change fee.
    Economy Flexi
    No change fee; fare difference only.

    Qantas

    Economy Saver or Sale
    Changes online for a fee plus fare difference.
    Economy Flex
    No change fee; fare difference only.

    British Airways

    Economy Basic
    Most restrictive; avoid if dates may move.
    Economy Standard or Plus
    Changes for a fee plus fare difference.
    Economy Flex
    No change fee; fare difference only.

    For one-stop Manchester options on other airlines, check that airline's own fare rules. Fare rules change; confirm them before paying.

    Where these numbers come from

    Every price on this page was returned by Google Flights in an automatic search. Nothing is estimated or typed in by hand.

    Prices in Singapore dollars for one adult in economy. Sold-out flights simply drop off the list until they reappear.
